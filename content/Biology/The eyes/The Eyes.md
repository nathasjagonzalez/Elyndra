#Biology 
*"Your eye is not a camera, It's a living biological supercomputer.*
*Light enters through the cornea, passes the pupil and gets focused by the lens. The image lands upside down on the retina, where millions of rods and cones convert light into electrical signals. These signals travel through the optic nerve... straight to your brain. You don't see with your eyes. You see with your [[BRAIN]]."*
