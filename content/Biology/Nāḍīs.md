**Key Aspects of Nāḍīs:**

- **Definition & Function:** Derived from the Sanskrit root _nad_ ("to flow"), nāḍīs are channels for psychic, vital, and spiritual energy that nourish every cell.
- **The Three Primary Channels:**

    - **Ida (Left):** Associated with the parasympathetic nervous system, cooling, mental energy, and the moon.
    - **Pingala (Right):** Associated with the sympathetic nervous system, heating, vital energy, and the sun.
    - **Sushumna (Central):** The central channel responsible for spiritual awareness and Kundalini activation.

- **Origins:** All nāḍīs are said to originate from two main centers: the heart and the _kanda_ (a bulbous structure below the navel).
- **Purification & Balance:** When blocked, energy is sluggish; techniques like _Prāṇāyāma_ (breathwork) and _Hatha Yoga_ are used to cleanse them.
- **Significance:** A balanced flow between Ida and Pingala allows energy to enter the central Sushumna channel, leading to higher consciousness. 

While some texts, such as the _Śivasaṃhitā_, suggest 350,000 nāḍīs, the commonly cited figure in [Haṭhayogapradīpikā](https://www.yogaspirit.com.au/about/blog/nadis) is 72,000
