“God” is not a being. It’s an experience. The English word **“God”** is a blunt knife. It flattens meaning. Original languages were _alive._ Whole cosmologies inside each sound. 

In Proto-Indo-European:  
***ǵʰu-tó-** = “that which is called / invoked.”  
Something you call on, not someone sitting on a throne.

In Old Norse:  
**Goð** = the unseen forces moving within fate.

In Sanskrit:  
**Deva** = “that which shines.”

Notice, never a man in the sky. Not a singular ruler. Not even a _person._ **A phenomenon. A presence. A light.**

Elohim ≠ “God” (singular). It means “the Divine many.” **Elohim** is plural.**-im** = “many.”

“Elohim” literally means:
The shining ones. The divine powers. The ones who shape reality. One word. Infinite deities. A collective consciousness, not a single dude with a beard. So why do most translations turn it into “God” (one being)? Because someone wanted control. Plural gods are hard to weaponize. One god? Easier to point at, package, manipulate. But the original text? It’s MULTI-DIMENSIONAL. Elohim is the choir. The One behind it all? That comes next.

YHWH (יהוה): The unsayable name. 
This is the most misunderstood word in history. YHWH isn’t a name like “Bob.” It’s not even a word. It’s a **breath pattern.**

**Yod – Heh – Vav – Heh**

Try to say it… You can’t say it. You can only **breathe it.** It sounds like: **inhale–exhale–inhale–exhale.**

YHWH is the sound of breathing. The pulse of existence. The “I AM.” Not a person, Not a sky-father. **Being itself.** This was holy. intimate. Saying it was like placing your mouth directly on the pulse of creation. So what happened? Why did “YHWH” become “Adonai” or “LORD”? The scribes thought: “This is too sacred. People might misuse it. Better to hide it.” So they replaced it in text with **Adonai** = “My Lord / My Master.”

But over time, people forgot there was even a name beneath the title. YHWH became LORD. The breath became a boss. The Infinite became a character. And eventually… that character was used to justify holy war, control, empire, fear. They took the **breath of existence** and turned it into a **middle manager.** Before being hijacked by empire YHWH was the name for the **living field.** The self-aware fabric of reality. Not male, not female. Not one, not many. **Is-ness.** The One behind the Elohim. The Source that births the gods.
