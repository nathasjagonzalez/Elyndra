**Geometric Connections**
- **Shapes & Angles**: Mudras and dance poses create tangible geometric forms like triangles, squares, circles, and line segments, illustrating angles (acute, right) and symmetry in a visual way.
- **Symmetry & Balance**: Classical Indian dance emphasizes perfect symmetry, balance, and alignment, requiring precise physical positioning that demonstrates mathematical principles of equilibrium and proportion.
- **Transformation**: Dancers transform between poses, showing how geometric figures can change while maintaining underlying mathematical rules.
